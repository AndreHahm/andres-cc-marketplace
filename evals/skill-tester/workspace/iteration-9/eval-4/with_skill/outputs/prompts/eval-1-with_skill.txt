# Note Formatter

## Rules

1. One output line per input bullet: `- [<TYPE>] <Summary> (#<id>)`.
2. `<TYPE>` is one of FEAT, FIX, DOCS, CHORE. Map "feature" to FEAT, "bug" to FIX, "docs" to DOCS,
   anything else to CHORE.
3. `<Summary>` is sentence case, has no trailing period, and is at most 60 characters.
4. A bullet without an issue number gets `(#none)`.
5. Group lines in the order FEAT, FIX, DOCS, CHORE, keeping input order inside a group.

## Testing & Validation

1. Mixed types are grouped in the documented order.
2. A bullet without an issue number is rendered with `(#none)`.
